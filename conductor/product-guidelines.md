# Product Guidelines — Disposable Temp Mail

## Voice and tone

- Professional, direct, neutral English across UI, docs, and code.
- No slang, profanity, or culturally loaded terms in user-facing strings,
  examples, or generated address word lists.
- Prefer concrete verbs and plain sentences over promotional language.

## UX principles

- Anonymous by default: no login, session scoped inboxes.
- Minimal actions: create (custom or random), copy, refresh, delete from session.
- Clear empty and error states; never expose other sessions' data.
- Consistent English UI strings; domain selector only when multiple domains exist.
