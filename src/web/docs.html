How to use Disposable Temp Mail

01

What it is

Disposable Temp Mail gives you anonymous email inboxes that need no account and no sign-up. Use one to receive confirmation codes, newsletters, or anything you don't want tied to your real address — then let it expire, or strike it when done.

02

Filing a new entry

Type a name and press Create for a custom address, or leave the field empty and press Create for a random one. Pick how long to keep it: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or until you remove it.

Every new entry comes with a transfer code. Copy it with the Code button — entering it on another device links the same inbox there.

03

Keeping & striking

  • Renew restarts an inbox's clock from today.
  • Plan changes how long an inbox is kept.
  • Strike from ledger deletes a single message.
  • Remove unlinks an inbox from your browser (its messages stay in the archive until expiry).

Keyboard: N files a new entry, R refreshes the register.

04

Fair-use limits

To keep the service free for everyone: 30 new inboxes per hour per network address, 20 per browser session, plus an automatic bot check on creation. Inboxes past their plan are purged nightly.

Install it as an app from your browser's address bar — it works offline as a shell and syncs when you're back.
