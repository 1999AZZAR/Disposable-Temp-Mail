§ 1

The service

Disposable Temp Mail provides free, anonymous, receive-only email inboxes. No account is required. Inboxes expire automatically according to their retention plan (7, 30, or 90 days, or until you remove them), and expired inboxes and their messages are permanently deleted.

You agree not to use the service for anything unlawful, including fraud, phishing, impersonation, harassment, or circumventing another service's security or eligibility controls. Automated bulk creation of inboxes beyond the published fair-use limits is not permitted.

§ 3

Abuse & enforcement

We may rate-limit, block, or delete inboxes and messages involved in abuse, with or without notice. Because inboxes are anonymous, enforcement happens at the level of addresses, network identifiers, and service-wide limits — never at the level of personal identity, because we hold none.
