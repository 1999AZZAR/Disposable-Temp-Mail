Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

§ 1

What we store

  • Inbox addresses you create or link, plus which anonymous browser session they belong to.
  • Received messages — sender, subject, body, and arrival time — kept only until the parent inbox expires or is removed.
  • Rate-limit records — network identifiers with timestamps, kept briefly to enforce fair-use limits and then discarded.

We collect no names, no emails of your own, no accounts, and no passwords — there is nothing to sign up with, so there is nothing to leak about you.

§ 2

Third parties

Traffic is served through Cloudflare, which processes requests as our hosting and security provider. Inbox creation passes a Cloudflare bot check. There is no advertising, no analytics beacon, and no tracker on these pages — nothing is sold or shared.

§ 3

Retention

Each inbox lives exactly as long as its plan says — 7, 30, or 90 days from creation or last renewal, or until you remove it. Expired inboxes, their messages, and their transfer codes are permanently deleted by a nightly purge. Struck messages are deleted immediately.

§ 4

Your rights

Deletion is self-service and instant: strike a message to delete it, remove an inbox to unlink it, and let expiry purge the rest. Because we hold no identity records, access or portability requests have nothing to attach to — if you can open the inbox in your browser, it is yours; otherwise it is nobody's.
